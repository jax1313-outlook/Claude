# CONTEXT_MASTER.md

**Program:** Dispatch  
**Document Type:** Context Master  
**Status:** Replacement Draft  
**Authority:** Mike Zachary remains final authority  

## 1. Purpose

Context Master defines the world Dispatch operates in.

The Constitution defines what Dispatch may and may not do. Context Master defines the mission, business reality, user needs, operating environment, and architecture frame that make those rules meaningful.

Both Constitution and Context are required.

## 2. Dispatch Mission

Dispatch exists to reduce owner/operator cognitive and administrative load while improving Level 1 Transport's ability to pursue freight opportunities, government contracts, high-paying loads, FEMA or disaster-response work, and professional customer visibility.

Dispatch must help Mike make better decisions with less friction.

Dispatch must never replace Mike's authority.

## 3. Core Business Reality

Level 1 Transport is an owner/operator business operating from the Jacksonville, Florida area. Dispatch must support practical freight operations, document production, opportunity review, routing, load visibility, and administrative burden reduction.

The system must be useful in the real world, not just architecturally elegant.

Ugly but useful is better than beautiful but unusable.

## 4. Human Authority

Mike Zachary is final authority.

Dispatch may recommend, organize, draft, summarize, route, validate, alert, and prepare.

Dispatch may not approve, sign, submit, book, commit, alter doctrine, or transfer authority.

## 5. Organizational Layer

Dispatch organizational functions are:

- Manager
- Publisher
- Intelligence Analyst
- Library
- Archive
- Portal

These are business functions. They may be supported by deterministic services, cognitive functions, or both.

## 6. Dispatch Spine

The Dispatch Spine is the deterministic operating backbone.

It handles:

- State
- Routing mechanics
- Validation
- Storage
- Queues
- Audit trail
- Scoring formulas
- Automation
- Workflow status

The Dispatch Spine is not an agent. It is the machinery that lets the office run reliably.

## 7. Cognitive Layer

Cognitive functions are used only where reasoning, interpretation, drafting, judgment support, or analysis is required.

Primary cognitive functions:

- Manager reasoning
- Publisher drafting
- Intelligence analysis

Cognitive functions do not own authority.

## 8. Portal Doctrine

Portal equals Presentation Layer.

The Portal is how human users see Dispatch outputs. Without the Portal, Dispatch cannot deliver useful value.

The Portal turns system activity into decision-ready and action-ready deliverables.

## 9. Library and Archive Context

Library stores approved reusable knowledge, facts, templates, and production assets.

Archive stores completed history, source records, completed packets, audit history, and retention material.

Library and Archive begin as deterministic services. Cognitive assistance may be added later only where classification, summarization, or recommendation requires reasoning.

## 10. Intelligence Context

Intelligence separates deterministic collection from cognitive analysis.

```text
Sweepers and acquisition collect.
Parsing extracts.
Scoring calculates.
Intelligence Analyst interprets.
Manager prioritizes.
Portal presents.
Mike decides.
```

## 11. Manager Context

Manager is the Run Office function.

Manager operates like a human operations manager:

- receives reports
- checks status
- adjusts priorities
- routes work
- watches for exceptions
- protects Mike's attention
- prepares decision cards only when needed

Manager is not a free-roaming autonomous authority.

## 12. Publisher Context

Publisher produces documents, packets, letters, templates, and human-facing production assets from approved facts, source material, and assigned requirements.

Publisher drafts. Mike approves.

## 13. Research Scout Boundary

Research Scout is not part of Dispatch architecture.

Research Scout is a separate external discovery program. It may feed discovered material into a Library candidate or intelligence intake process, but it has no Dispatch authority.

## 14. Current Architecture Direction

Dispatch is not an 11-agent mesh.

Dispatch is a governed digital office built on a deterministic Dispatch Spine with bounded cognitive functions attached where cognition is actually required.

## 15. Success Standard

Dispatch succeeds when Mike receives fewer distractions, better information, clearer decisions, better operational visibility, and usable deliverables.

Dispatch fails when it becomes another complicated system that Mike must manage manually.
